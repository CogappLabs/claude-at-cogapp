## Don't do this

- Pasting `.env` contents to "share context" (Claude _will_ try to access this file)
- Blanket-approving wildcards: `Bash(git *)`, `Bash(npm *)`, etc
- Reflexively approving prompts
- Adding MCP servers or skills without vetting them
- Routinely bypassing safety gates: `--no-verify`
- Pointing it at production credentials when dev/staging would do
